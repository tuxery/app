# Accessibility

Tuxery should be usable by everyone, whatever the way they browse: with
a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier, high contrast or reduced
motion. This page says where the site stands today, honestly.

## Target

[WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) level **AA**, in both the light
and the dark theme.

**Current status: partially conformant.** Some parts of the site don't
meet the target yet; they are listed below.

## What was checked

Audit of 9 October 2026, on the homepage, search results, an app page
(Firefox), the apps, games and categories pages, settings, a store page,
a creator page and the docs pages:

- **Automated checks** with [axe-core](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core)
  (WCAG 2.0/2.1 A and AA rules, plus best practices), in the light and
  dark themes, at desktop and phone widths.
- **Keyboard navigation**: every link, button and menu reached with
  `Tab`, in a logical order, with a visible focus indicator.
- **Screen reader labels**: icon-only buttons (menu, settings, account,
  close) have a text label; lists such as the catalog pipeline are real
  lists; pages have one `h1` and landmarks (header, navigation, main,
  footer).
- **Reduced motion**: smooth scrolling is turned off when your system
  asks for less motion.

Fixed during this audit: menu items had no visible keyboard focus; the
inert account button and footer headings used invalid markup.

Fixed since: **color contrast**. Links, secondary text, the green
"Verified" badges, section and table headings were below the 4.5:1 ratio
WCAG AA asks for normal text. The light theme's blue and green are now
deeper, secondary text is never fainter than 70% of the text color, and
the automated checks report no contrast issue in either theme, background
photo included (re-checked 9 October 2026, 18 pages).

Fixed since: the **screenshot row** on app pages couldn't be reached from
the keyboard; each screenshot is now a button opening a full-size viewer
(arrow keys to browse, Escape to close).

## Known gaps

- **Accessible names that differ from the visible text** on category
  tiles, a homepage call to action, and the Edition/Version pickers of an
  app page: a voice-control user saying the visible label may not reach
  them.
- **Headings** skip a level in search results.
- **Not checked yet**: a full manual test with screen readers (Orca,
  NVDA, VoiceOver), 200% zoom and text spacing, Windows high-contrast
  mode, and the install drawer's focus handling.

These are tracked as work to do; this page will be updated as they are
fixed.

## Report a problem

If something on Tuxery is hard or impossible for you to use, please tell
us — it is a bug, and a priority one. Open an issue on the
[app repository](https://github.com/tuxery/app/issues/new) describing
the page, what you tried to do and what assistive technology you use,
if any. Data problems (an app's description or screenshots) go to the
[catalog repository](https://github.com/tuxery/catalog/issues/new).

## Legal context

In France, an accessibility statement following the RGAA is required of
public bodies and large companies. Tuxery is neither: this statement is
voluntary, written because it is good practice and because we want to
know where we stand.
