# Roadmap

All of Tuxery's planned work is tracked in the open, as cards on the
[Tuxery GitHub Project](https://github.com/orgs/tuxery/projects/1) —
features, data fixes and technical chores alike. There is no separate,
private list.

## Reading the board

Each card sits in one column:

| Status          | Means                                                       |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Backlog**     | An idea or a known gap, written down so it isn't forgotten. |
| **Todo**        | Decided, and next in line.                                  |
| **In Progress** | Someone is working on it now.                               |
| **Done**        | Shipped — see the [changelog](/docs/changelog/).            |

The **Codebase** field says where the work happens: `app` (this site),
`catalog` (the data pipeline), or both. Many cards are internal (database
quotas, CI); the themes below are the ones you'll notice on the site.

## What's coming

### Better data

- More sources, and fuller ones: Flathub's beta branch, every Snap
  channel, the stable releases of openSUSE (Leap), Nixpkgs and Slackware,
  AppImageHub. Each is listed on the [per-source to-do](/docs/coverage/).
- Fewer apps left "To Classify", and categories split more finely
  (messaging and social apps apart from browsers and email; apps for
  kids).
- Spotting distribution packages that differ from the official build,
  and outdated versions.

### More useful app pages

- Technical details: runtime, architectures, download and installed size.
- A safety summary for sandboxed apps (Flatpak permissions).
- The install method the app's own developers recommend.
- Alternatives: other apps that do the same job.

### Taking part

- Flagging a wrong merge or proposing one from the site.
- Submitting an app or game, and a way for developers to claim their
  app's page.
- Votes, to surface apps no source ranks.
- Creator pages for anyone who wants to share the apps they use.
- A personal space to keep your filters and follow your installs.

### Open data

- The merged catalog as a download, then a public API — see
  [open data](/docs/open-data/).

## Weigh in

Want something moved up, or missing from the list? Open an issue on the
[app](https://github.com/tuxery/app/issues) or
[catalog](https://github.com/tuxery/catalog/issues) repository, or
comment on an existing one. Real use cases are what decide priorities.
